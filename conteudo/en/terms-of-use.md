## 1. Minimum age

**Nimo Chat is intended for people aged 18 and over.** By creating an account
you declare that you are 18 or older. We don't allow accounts for minors, and
any that are identified are deleted without prior notice.

If you suspect you are talking to a minor, **report it immediately** through

top priority.

## 2. How the account works

Your account is anonymous: we don't ask for an email address, phone number or
password. That has a consequence you need to understand:

**If you uninstall the app without saving your recovery code, you may lose the
account forever.** We store only a fingerprint of the code, so not even we can
recover it. Generate yours in Profile → Account → Recovery code.

You are responsible for keeping that code safe. Anyone who has the code can
access your account.

## 3. Community rules

Nimo Chat exists for talking to strangers. That only works if everyone
behaves. The following is **prohibited**:

**Zero tolerance — immediate and permanent suspension:**
- Any content involving minors, especially of a sexual nature
- Nudity or sexual content sent without the other person having asked for it
- Threats, incitement to violence or glorification of crime
- Sharing other people's personal data without authorization (doxxing)
- Recording or sharing conversations and calls without consent

**Also prohibited:**

## 4. Your responsibility for what you send

You are solely responsible for what you write, send and post. We don't read
your conversations: text messages are not reviewed by a human, only checked
by an automated keyword filter. Photos you send go through an additional
automated check (no human review) that looks exclusively for
maximum-confidence signals of explicit nudity or graphic violence; when
detected, the content is automatically removed and the account is flagged
for human review.

By sending content, you declare that you have the right to do so and you grant
us a limited license, solely to store and deliver that content within the app.
**The content remains yours.** We don't use your photos, messages or videos
for any other purpose.

## 5. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Every conversation has a report button and a block button. **We review every
report within 24 hours.**

What can happen to anyone who breaks these rules: content removal, a warning,
a temporary suspension or a permanent ban — depending on how serious it is.

Reports involving **a suspected minor, unsolicited nudity or a threat** get
top priority and go into an immediate suspension flow, before human review.

> To be exact about what the system does: automatic suspension is triggered by
> a **report**, not by analysis of the content. We don't read or scan messages
> looking for violations — and we're not going to promise monitoring that
> doesn't exist.

There is no subscription refund in the case of a ban for breaking the rules.

If you disagree with a decision, write to suporte@chatnimo.com and we will
review it.

## 6. About view-once media

The app lets you send photos and videos that are deleted from our server as
soon as they are opened. Be clear-eyed about what that protects:

- On Android, we block screen capture while the media is open.
- **On iPhone there is no technical way to prevent screenshots.** Apple
  doesn't offer that API to any app.
- The other person can always photograph the screen with another device.

**Don't send anything you can't afford to lose control of.**

## 7. Voice calls

Calls only happen between people who have accepted each other as friends. The
audio goes directly from one device to the other and is not recorded by us.

Recording a call without the other person's consent is prohibited by these
terms and may be a crime.
