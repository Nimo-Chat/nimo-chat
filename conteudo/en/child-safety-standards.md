# Child Safety Standards — Nimo Chat

**Last updated:** August 21, 2026

Nimo Chat has **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE) — in any form, on any account, without exception. This document
describes our standards, how to report a problem, and what we do once a
report comes in.

Please also read our [Terms of Use](terms.html) and
[Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

---

## 1. Minimum age

**Nimo Chat is intended exclusively for people 18 and older.** We do not
allow accounts belonging to minors under any circumstance. When creating an
account, a person declares they are 18 or older — accounts identified as
belonging to a minor are suspended and removed.

## 2. What is prohibited

The following is prohibited without exception:

- Sending, requesting, or sharing any image, video, audio, or text that
  sexualizes, exploits, or endangers a child or teenager.
- Using Nimo Chat to groom, contact, or attempt to meet a minor with any
  sexual or exploitative intent.
- Pretending to be a minor, or encouraging someone else to pretend, as part
  of any of the conduct described above.
- Sharing links, external contacts, or instructions that direct someone to
  this kind of content outside the app.

Any account involved is permanently banned, and the case may be reported to
the relevant authorities.

## 3. How to report

Every conversation and every post on Nimo Chat has a report button. When
reporting, there is a specific reason — **"Minor"** — made for exactly this
situation. You can also write directly to **abuso@chatnimo.com**.

Reports involving a suspected minor get top priority: if the two people
actually had a conversation together, the reported account is
**automatically suspended on the spot**, before any manual review even
happens. That exists precisely so an urgent case never has to wait on
human review alone.

## 4. What happens after a report

- **Suspected minor with a confirmed shared conversation** → immediate
  automatic suspension of the reported account.
- **Any account reported by three different people**, for any reason, is
  automatically suspended, even before manual review.
- **Severe reports** (minor, nudity, or violence) or **two or more
  reports** against the same account go into a human review queue.
- Someone from our moderation team reviews the queue and decides: keep the
  ban (permanent) or clear the account if the report doesn't hold up.
- Banned accounts are removed from the matchmaking queue — they can no
  longer talk to anyone in the app.

Photos and videos sent in chat are **view-once**: they disappear after
being seen. That doesn't prevent a report — what the person saw can be
described in the report's details field.

## 5. Cooperation with authorities

We retain a banned account's data for as long as needed to respond to
lawful requests from authorities. Cases of child exploitation are reported
to the relevant agencies — in Brazil, to the Federal Police and/or
SaferNet Brasil; outside Brazil, to the National Center for Missing &
Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the equivalent authority in the person's
country.

## 6. Point of contact

For questions about these standards, or to report a child safety issue
directly without going through the app, write to
**abuso@chatnimo.com**. This inbox is monitored by the person responsible
for Nimo Chat and for compliance with Google Play's CSAE policies.
